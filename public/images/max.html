Jewellery has always been integral to my life and is my passion. Inspired by the traditional cultures and people that I have met in my life of travelling. I want each piece to tell a story infused with symbolism and talismanic powers.

I create treasures that you will want to hand down to your children. Each piece is hand made; an eclectic mix of jewels found on my travels to remote places, historical pieces in their entirety or reset in my designs using silver, gold, fossils and gemstones.

Bespoke Collection

Sally Dudmesh Bespoke, is a range of very special, unique work.

Each piece is a one-off, with a story, an eclectic mix of inspirations and visual language inspired by Sally’s travels to remote places and the cultures she has encountered and fallen in love with along the way. Exotic rocks and precious stones set in gold, antique talismans from around the world adorn hand made chains, creating jewellery that feels as mythical and magical as it is beautiful.

For further details and prices, please contact Sally directly.

sally@sallydudmesh.com

Inspired by Africa

While Sally was born and travelled extensively in Asia, Africa has been her home for the last three decades. She has lived, like Karen Bixen, at the foot of the Ngong Hills, and spent a number of very happy years on the flamingo-fringed shores of Lake Elemntaita. Kenya’s wildlife and wilderness continue to be a source of breathtaking inspiration, which can all be seen in this wonderful collection.
